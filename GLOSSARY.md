# Custom Cost of Living Comparison

An individual compares personal living expenses across US cities using editable estimates, actual spending, and lifestyle choices. Comparisons support early-career adults and student scenarios without requiring current spending.

## Language

**Individual**:
The one person whose income and personal expenses are compared across cities. Shared living arrangements contribute only that person’s share of shared expenses.
_Avoid_: Household, family

**Current spending**:
The individual's actual living expenses in its current location, broken down by expense category.
_Avoid_: Average local spending

**Destination budget**:
The individual's estimated living expenses in a destination location, including any chosen lifestyle changes.
_Avoid_: Actual destination spending

**Lifestyle adjustment**:
A deliberate change to how the individual lives at the destination, such as choosing a smaller apartment or giving up a car.

**Cost of living comparison**:
A comparison of personal budgets for two or three US cities, using editable estimates or actual spending and the individual’s living preferences.

**Suggested destination cost**:
A location-based estimate for an expense category that the individual can replace with its own amount.
_Avoid_: Actual cost, guaranteed cost

**Fixed expense**:
An expense the individual chooses to keep at the same amount across locations, such as a debt payment.

**Custom category**:
A individual-defined expense category beyond the calculator's standard living expense categories.

**Take-home income**:
The individual's monthly income after taxes and payroll deductions, entered separately for each location.
_Avoid_: Gross salary

**Monthly remainder**:
Take-home income minus living expenses for a location. A negative amount means expenses exceed income.
_Avoid_: Savings

**Destination**:
A US location being considered for relocation, with its own destination budget and take-home income.

**Data area**:
The geographic area covered by a suggested cost's source data, which may be broader than the selected city.
_Avoid_: Exact city cost

**Housing cost**:
The individual’s monthly housing expense, including their chosen share of rent in a shared apartment. Total apartment rent and personal rent share are distinct amounts; splits may be unequal and editable by city.

**Living preferences**:
An individual’s choices affecting personal expenses, such as living alone or with roommates, using a car or transit, and discretionary spending.

**Saved comparison**:
A named cost of living comparison retained in the individual's browser, which can be exported to a file and imported again.

**Incomplete budget**:
A budget with at least one applicable expense category still missing an amount. A missing amount is not a zero expense.

**Manual override**:
A individual-entered amount that replaces a suggested destination cost and remains in place until the individual changes it or explicitly restores the suggestion.

**Moving cost**:
A one-time relocation expense, kept separate from recurring living expenses.

**Moving-cost recovery period**:
The number of months of lower recurring living expenses needed to recover the moving cost. It applies only when destination living expenses are lower than current spending.

**Salary benchmark**:
A sourced median or average annual earnings or wage reference, separate from personal budget income. City-resident earnings and metro occupational wages are distinct populations and are never blended.
_Avoid_: Personal income, expected job offer

**University discovery**:
Information about universities within a chosen straight-line radius of a comparison city’s center, initially 25 miles, including names, locations, distances, and websites. Distance is not commute time; tuition is a separate, individually entered expense.

**Savings goal**:
An individual’s planned monthly amount to set aside, shown separately from living expenses and compared with the monthly remainder.
_Avoid_: Living expense, guaranteed savings

**Supported city**:
A city in the curated launch coverage with sourced and dated expense estimates. A source’s geographic coverage may be broader than the city and must be identified.

**Manual city budget**:
A personal budget for a city without supported estimates, using amounts entered by the individual. Missing amounts remain unknown rather than counting as zero.

**Comparison baseline**:
The shared living preferences initially applied to all cities in a comparison. City-specific changes are explicit departures from that baseline.

**Student funding**:
Money available to a student from work, grants, family support, and loan proceeds, with borrowing identified separately from non-borrowed funding.
_Avoid_: Salary

**Loan proceeds**:
Borrowed money available to pay expenses during the budget period. It is distinct from earnings and from debt repayments.

**Savings-goal coverage**:
Whether the monthly remainder is sufficient for the individual’s savings goal. Coverage is provisional when applicable expense amounts are missing.

**City-resident earnings**:
Annual pretax individual earnings of city residents aged 16 or older who worked full time, year round and had earnings. This reference population is not restricted to Gen Z.
_Avoid_: Household income, Gen Z salary

**Metro occupational wages**:
Wages for a defined occupation among covered jobs in a metropolitan area. This workplace-based benchmark is distinct from city-resident earnings.

**Gross rent benchmark**:
Median rent for occupied cash-rent units with a specified bedroom count, including utilities covered by the source’s gross-rent definition. It is a historical resident benchmark, not a current asking-rent quote; included utilities must not be counted twice.

**Eligible institution**:
A two-year or four-year college or university included in university discovery. Standalone trade schools and non-degree providers are outside the initial discovery scope.

**Funding period**:
The number of months that a student’s semester or annual funding must cover. Period-based funding and expenses are converted to monthly equivalents over the chosen period.

**Broader-area estimate**:
An expense estimate sourced from a region or national population rather than the selected city. Its actual geography, source, and period remain visible.
